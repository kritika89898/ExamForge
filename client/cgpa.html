SGPA = Σ(Credits × Grade points) ÷ Σ Credits. Division: 8.5 and above First with Distinction, 6.0 – 8.49 First, 5.0 – 5.99 Second, below 5.0 Fail. Courses graded F aren't counted in CGPA, so leave their credits out of a semester's total.
